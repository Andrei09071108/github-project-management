# Prioritisation

High covers prerequisites or urgent triage needs. Medium improves day-to-day retrieval. Low covers useful reporting or deferred discovery. Rank dependencies ahead of their consumers; a High item can remain in Backlog while blocked. Estimate uses approximate hours of effort. Dates and estimates are fictional planning assumptions. Labels describe type or impediment; priority and delivery state live in Project fields. Review ageing work by reading its latest evidence and unresolved dependencies.
